The App

Fresh material.
A real way to practice it.

Riffology writes riffs for you to play, then helps you work them up and understand what's going on under your fingers.

Discover your next riff

Pick your level, key and feel, then tap New. Every riff is generated on your phone, so there's always another phrase you wouldn't have played.

Create

Build it up at your pace

Loop a bar, slow the tempo, and follow a guided Learn, Build and Play drill. The metronome and count-in help you settle into the rhythm.

Practice

Understand what you play

See the riff on the fretboard with every note's role colored in, from the root to the flat seventh. Then dig into its scale, rhythm and phrase structure, with playing tips for the riff in front of you.

Explore

A new focus every day

The Today tab hands you a themed set of fresh riffs with one clear musical idea to work on, and keeps track of your practice time and daily progress.

Today

Hear where your chords lead

Collect chord shapes in the Chord Armory, line them up into a progression and play it back. Hold a card for chords that pair well with it.

Chord Armory

Keep the ideas that stick

Save the riffs you love to your Library, come back to them when you practice, and share any riff's audio with a bandmate.

Library
The Practice Drill

Slow it down.
Build it up.

Every riff comes with the method a teacher would give you: learn the hard bar slowly, bring the tempo back up over the whole riff, then play it through once at speed.

1 Learn

The hard bar at 55%

Riffology finds the bar that'll give you the most trouble and loops it slowly, with a playing tip to focus on.

4 reps, one bar on loop
2 Build

Whole riff, 70% → 85%

Now the whole thing, getting quicker. If a bar falls apart, drop back a rung. Speed you can't hold isn't speed yet.

2 reps at each tempo
3 Play

Full tempo, once

One clean pass at full speed. Play it like you mean it, not like you're checking it.

100%, no safety net
