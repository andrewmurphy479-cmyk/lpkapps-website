The Game

Cute on the outside.
Tennis to the bone.

No ads, no energy timers, no loot boxes. Just the sport — served by blobs.

Honest physics

Topspin dips, slice skids, lobs float over a rushing net player. Every bounce comes out of a real simulation — so winners are earned, never gifted.

Scouting Reports

Know your enemy.

Nine rivals stand between you and the title. Every one has a habit — and every habit can be exploited. Straight from the in-game scouting cards.

Mo

The Moonballer

Floats everything to the moon. Step in and take it early — or just smash.

Bea

The Backboard

Runs down everything, hurts nothing. Pull her in with drops — she lives on the baseline.

Cal

The Cannon

First serve like a freight train, second serve like a gift. Block the first back, feast on the second.

Dash

The Retriever

Fastest feet in the city, slices that stay shin-high. Stretch him corner to corner — he breaks before he misses.

Skye

The Lob Queen

The instant you lean toward the net, the lob is already up. Keep your overhead loaded.

Rush

The Volley

Serves, charges, volleys — every point at your throat. Dip the pass low or throw the lob.

Ivy

The Wall

She does not miss. Every point must be ended twice — take the net and finish it yourself.

Boom

The Ace Machine

Biggest serve on tour and a hammer behind it. Get the return down at his feet — the second serve is your window.

Nova

The Legend

No weakness but one: camp the baseline and she will drop you. Everything you have learned, all at once.
